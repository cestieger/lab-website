In January 2027, the Stieger Lab starts as a Junior Research Group at the Technical University of Munich, supported by a Liebig Fellowship from the Fonds der Chemischen Industrie (FCI).

The lab works at the interface of synthetic chemistry, chemical biology and drug discovery. We develop chemical tools that modulate oncogenic transcription, combining new covalent chemistries, high-throughput ligand discovery and genome-wide profiling of chromatin regulators.

The lab builds on Christian's research at the Leibniz-Forschungsinstitut für Molekulare Pharmakologie (FMP) in Berlin and at the University of California, Berkeley.
