<div class="u-title">
  <h2>Chemistry to Control Transcription</h2>
  <span class="u-byline">Chemical tools to modulate oncogenic transcription</span>
</div>

<p class="u-intro">The Stieger Lab works at the interface of synthetic chemistry, chemical biology and drug discovery. Our long-term goal is to develop new therapeutic strategies against cancer and to gain a deeper understanding of how chromatin regulation shapes gene expression.</p>

<div class="u-boxes three">
  <div class="u-box">

    <h3>Covalent chemistries</h3>
    <span class="u-byline">Targeting proteins</span>
    <p>We develop new covalent chemistries to target proteins selectively and to build modular chemical tools.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="u-box">
    <div class="u-icon"><i class="fa-solid fa-vials"></i></div>
    <h3>Peptide ligand discovery</h3>
    <span class="u-byline">High-throughput screening</span>
    <p>We discover peptide and peptidomimetic ligands in high throughput to reach proteins that small molecules struggle to bind.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="u-box">
    <div class="u-icon"><i class="fa-solid fa-dna"></i></div>
    <h3>Chromatin profiling</h3>
    <span class="u-byline">Genome-wide readouts</span>
    <p>We profile chromatin regulators genome-wide to understand and reprogram oncogenic gene expression.</p>
