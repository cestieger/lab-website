Chemical Biology and Induced Proximity for Novel Therapeutics

The Stieger Lab at the Technical University of Munich works at the interface of synthetic chemistry, chemical biology and drug discovery.

Our research

Research

Chemical tools to modulate oncogenic transcription

We combine new covalent chemistries, high-throughput discovery of peptide and peptidomimetic ligands, and genome-wide profiling of chromatin regulators to design chemical tools to modulate oncogenic transcription.

Our long-term goal is to develop new therapeutic strategies against cancer and to gain a deeper understanding of how chromatin regulation shapes gene expression.

  • Covalent chemistries

    New covalent chemistries for targeting proteins.

  • Peptide ligand discovery

    High-throughput discovery of peptide and peptidomimetic ligands.

  • Chromatin profiling

    Genome-wide profiling of chromatin regulators and gene expression.
